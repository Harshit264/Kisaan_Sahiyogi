Kisaan Sahiyogi

Welcome to Kisaan Sahiyogi. A website specifically made to cater to the needs of the farmers of India. The goal of this website is to prevent misuse of any chemical or industrial products used by farmers in everyday life, often leading to catastrophic amounts of financial and health loss due to a lack of awareness or simply because there is no information available out in the open in everyday language for farmers to read and understand the practical implications of the products used.
